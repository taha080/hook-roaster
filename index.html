How the score works

Hook Roaster weighs five signals, 20 points each: curiosity, clarity, specificity, emotion, and length / scannability. It looks for power words, numbers, questions, filler, clichés, line length, and whether the reader shows up as “you.” Rewrites are templates filled with the topic, role, and numbers it finds in your opener.
